Recipe

15 min

Avocado power bowl

420 kcal · serves 1 · 15 min

Vegan High fibre Goal friendly

Protein

28g

Carbs

34g

Fat

19g

Ingredients

  • Ripe avocado1 whole · 240 kcal
  • Cooked quinoa120 g · 130 kcal
  • Cherry tomatoes80 g · 15 kcal
  • Pumpkin seeds10 g · 35 kcal

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the quinoa and let it cool for a few minutes.

  2. 2

    Halve the avocado, scoop it out and slice it lengthways.

  3. 3

    Quarter the tomatoes and fold them through the quinoa.

  4. 4

    Top with avocado and pumpkin seeds, then season to taste.
