Key flows
prototype

Ten linked screens: the two core journeys plus the Stats and You tabs. Click any step to enter the flow, then use the in-screen back arrow and bottom navigation to move around exactly as you would in the app. Every screen renders from tokens.css and components.css — the same files that drive the styleguide.
